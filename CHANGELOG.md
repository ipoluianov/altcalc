# Changelog

## Unreleased

The first version.

### Added

- Expressions typed with the result shown as they are typed; the colors of
  the tokens, the error underlined, the missing parentheses added.
- Exact integers up to ±9.2·10¹⁸, 15 significant digits for the others, a
  result put back into an expression with its full precision.
- Operators + − × ÷ ^ √ ! % mod ² ³ °, implicit multiplication (2π, 3(4+5)),
  percent as on a calculator.
- Functions: trigonometric, inverse and hyperbolic in degrees, radians or
  gradians; roots, exponents, logarithms; rounding; min, max, sum, avg,
  median; gcd, lcm, factorial, combinations; rand.
- Hex, binary and octal numbers, bitwise operators, the integer results in
  hex, octal and binary; fractions shown as num/den.
- Variables and ans, kept between the starts.
- History of the calculations, kept between the starts; Up and Down recall
  the expressions.
- Memory keys MC, MR, M+, M−, MS.
- Standard and scientific keypads, "2nd" for the inverse functions.
- Copy of the result or the whole calculation, paste of numbers written
  in the US or the European way.
- Light and dark themes, English and Russian, decimal point or comma,
  digit grouping, always on top, quick reference (F1).
- The fonts of the system: its monospace font for the numbers, its
  interface font for the keys, its symbol font for √, ∛, ⌫ and the like.
- One running copy: a second start shows the window of the first one.
- Windows self-install, Linux packages and installer script, macOS dmg,
  as in the other AltBins utilities.
